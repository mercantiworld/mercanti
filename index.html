What We Do

Mercantiworld designs and builds bespoke e-commerce platforms for brands that need more flexibility, more control, and more capability than standard SaaS systems can offer. Below is an overview of our core services.

1. Custom E-Commerce Platform Development

We build tailored commerce systems from the ground up no templates, no restrictions. Your platform is shaped entirely around your workflows, customer experience, and long-term plans.

Ideal for brands that need:

  • Unique buying journeys
  • Custom product logic or pricing
  • Multi-step or complex checkouts
  • Non-standard business models

2. Systems Integration

We connect your e-commerce system with the tools your business already relies on. Typical integrations include:

  • ERP, WMS, CRM, PIM
  • Inventory & fulfilment systems
  • Payment and subscription platforms
  • Custom internal software

3. Operational Workflow Design

We map and build your operational workflows into your system creating efficiency, automation, and fewer manual processes.

Examples:

  • Automated order routing
  • Custom fulfilment logic
  • Workflow approvals
  • B2B-specific sales processes

4. Long-Term Support & Scalability

Your business will evolve your platform should evolve with it. We offer ongoing iteration, enhancements, and performance improvements.

Our approach ensures:

  • No SaaS lock in
  • No forced upgrades
  • No dependency on plugins
  • Full ownership over your system
